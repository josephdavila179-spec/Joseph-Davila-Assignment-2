Brand New Urban Farm that Opens in Downtown Los Angeles

Sustainable Gardens in the Heart of a Community

Activists, community members, and many others appeared here on this Sunday morning to celebrate the official opening of Green Canopy Urban Farm located at the intersection of Grand Avenue and 9th Street. This project makes a two-acre parking lot into a community farm where people can learn about farming, grow fresh food, and bring the whole neighborhood together.

Put together by the local non-profit LA Green Horizons, and aims to help fight food shortages and give the youth volunteer opportunities locally. This site will show modern growing setups, gardens that are bee-friendly, and a composting facility. The first harvest of greens, tomatoes, and herbs can be expected by late fall.

Community Leaders Speak Out on Local Impact

During the opening ceremony the project director Elena Rostova began to address the crowd and mentioned how the garden is meant to bring the community together around sustainability.

“This garden is a place that is more than just growing food, it is a place that is designed to bring our community together through its shared purpose and sustainability.”

- Elena Rostova, Organizer at LA Green Horizons

In addition to growing food, the farm will also have weekly classes on cooking and gardening along with summer internships for high school students. Anyone can sign up until the end of the month to volunteer or to also reserve their own plot for gardening.
