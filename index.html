Brand New Urban Farm Opens in Downtown Los Angeles

Activists, community members, and many others appeared here on this Sunday morning to celebrate the official opening of Green Canopy Urban Farm located at the intersection of Grand Avenue and 9th Street. This project makes a two-acre parking lot into a community farm where people can learn about farming, grow fresh food, and bring the whole neighborhood together.
